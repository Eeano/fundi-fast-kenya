How Fundi Fast Kenya Works

For Customers:
1. Choose your Province → County → Trade
2. View a real fundi profile
3. Tap WhatsApp → Send enquiry
4. Agree price + time directly with fundi. Pay AFTER work.

For Fundis:
1. Fill Join Form below
2. Owner reviews and calls you to check your number
3. If approved, your profile is published
4. You get customer enquiries on WhatsApp. You pay KES 200 only AFTER you get work.

How Fundi Fast Kenya Works

For Customers:
1. Use "I Need a Fundi Now" OR Choose Province → County → Trade
2. You get a Job ID like FFK-124 - save it
3. View a real fundi profile with photos
4. Tap WhatsApp → Send enquiry
5. Agree price + time. Pay AFTER work.
6. Report DONE / NO SHOW with your Job ID

For Fundis:
1. Fill Join Form below
2. Owner reviews and calls you to check your number
3. If approved, your profile is published
4. You get enquiries. You pay KES 200 only AFTER job is confirmed DONE.

Trust & Safety

- Your fundi's phone number is NEVER shown on the website. You book via Fundi Fast Kenya.
- We do NOT show "Verified" unless we have checked it.
- Phone Checked = We called the number and spoke to the fundi.
- ⭐ Top Fundi = 3+ jobs reported DONE, no complaints.
- New Listing = Just joined, phone check pending.
- 2x NO SHOW reports = Fundi is removed.
- Always agree price, scope and time BEFORE work starts.
- Do not pay in advance unless you agree it yourself.
